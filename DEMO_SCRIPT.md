### [00:00 - 00:10] Opening Problem Hook
*(Visual: Quick shot of busy Indian street bazaar or shot of Dukaan Saathi dashboard with the hero "Boliye" mic button)*

### [00:10 - 00:25] The Live Voice Order Demo
*(Visual: Presenter clicks the large orange "Boliye" mic button on Dukaan Saathi)*

**Presenter (Speaks into mic):**  
*"Bhaiya, do kilo chawal aur ek litre refined oil chahiye."*

*(Visual: Pulsing audio ripple -> Status stepper shows: 'Listening' -> 'Gnani Prisma v2.5' -> 'Understanding Order' -> 'Checking Stock' -> 'Speaking Response')*

**Presenter (Voiceover):**  
"In under 400 milliseconds, Gnani Prisma v2.5 transcribes the speech. Our NLU extracts the quantities, units, and matches them to the store catalog."

---

### [00:25 - 00:38] The Spoken Hindi Response
*(Visual: Order card pops up displaying 2 kg Chawal (₹120) and 1 litre Refined Oil (₹180), Total ₹300. The app automatically plays the spoken Hindi audio)*

**Dukaan Saathi Spoken Voice (Gnani Timbre v2.5):**  
🔊 *"Aapka order: 2 kilo chawal aur 1 litre refined oil. Total ₹300. Confirm karein?"*

**Presenter (Voiceover):**  
"Notice how Gnani Timbre speaks back the exact bill in warm, natural Hindi so both the shopkeeper and customer hear the confirmation hands-free."

### [00:38 - 00:50] One-Click Confirm, Stock & Analytics Dashboard

**Presenter (Voiceover):**
